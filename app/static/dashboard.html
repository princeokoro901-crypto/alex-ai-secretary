Orchestration history & recovery

          
          Not loaded
        

        
Inspect recent runs, statuses, and results. For uncertain runs, authorized operators can record one immutable acknowledgement or escalation decision. This records human review only; it does not prove the external outcome, change task status, or retry work. Alex does not automatically retry failed or uncertain tasks. Before entering the runtime, Alex durably records a minimal request summary and idempotency key when persistent storage is configured. If a restart interrupts a run, Alex marks it uncertain and never automatically retries it. Task input values are not copied into the recovery summary; only their keys and a SHA-256 digest are recorded.
